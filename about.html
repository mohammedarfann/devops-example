A contractor firm, not a broker

Pragathi Constructions was started in 2008 by K. Rajesh Babu, a civil engineer from JNTU Hyderabad, who was tired of watching homeowners get passed between architects, contractors, and sub-contractors with no single point of accountability.

Our Story

Rooted in Hyderabad

We began with two site engineers and a single JCB on hire, doing boundary walls and small residential builds in Uppal and LB Nagar. Word spread because we showed up every day and did not disappear after collecting the advance.

By 2015 we had our own equipment yard in Miyapur and a permanent team of masons, bar benders, electricians, and plumbers. Today we run projects from Shamshabad to Medchal, though most of our work remains within the GHMC limits.

We are not the cheapest option in the market. We are the option for clients who want a written contract, transparent billing, and a site supervisor who picks up the phone.

Principles

What we will not compromise on

Material traceability

Every cement bag and steel lot is logged with delivery challans. Clients can inspect material records at any point during construction.

No subcontracting of core work

Structural concrete, masonry, and waterproofing are done by our own crews. We only bring in specialists for glass facades and elevator installation.

Written change orders

Any deviation from the approved plan — however small — is documented with cost and timeline impact before work proceeds.

Safety compliance

Full PPE for all workers, barricading at excavation sites, and weekly safety audits. Zero fatalities across 17 years of operation.
