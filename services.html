End-to-end contracting under one roof

We take projects from soil testing and foundation to keys-in-hand delivery. You get a single contract, a single invoice trail, and one person accountable for the entire build.

01

Residential Construction

Independent villas, duplex homes, and G+2/G+3 apartment buildings. We work from your architect's drawings or can recommend architects we have partnered with on 40+ Hyderabad projects.

  • Soil testing and foundation design
  • RCC frame structure with IS-standard reinforcement
  • Brickwork, plastering, and waterproofing
  • Basic electrical and plumbing rough-in
02

Commercial & Retail

Office floors, showrooms, restaurants, and warehouse shells. We understand GHMC commercial building norms and fire NOC requirements for occupied structures.

  • Shell-and-core construction
  • False ceiling and flooring
  • HVAC ducting coordination
  • Fire-rated partitions and exits
03

Renovation & Retrofitting

Older Hyderabad properties — especially pre-2000 constructions in Secunderabad and Old City — often need structural assessment before cosmetic upgrades. We diagnose first, then quote.

  • Structural crack assessment and repair
  • Terrace waterproofing and leakage treatment
  • Floor elevation and layout changes
  • Complete interior fit-out

Our Process

How a project moves forward

Site Visit & Brief

We visit your plot or existing property, review drawings, and understand budget and timeline expectations.

BOQ & Estimate

Itemised bill of quantities with material specifications, labour rates, and a milestone-based payment schedule.

Agreement & Mobilisation

Signed contract, advance receipt, material procurement, and site setup including labour camp if required.

Execution & Handover

Weekly progress updates, stage-wise inspections, snag-list resolution, and final occupancy handover with warranties.

Materials

What goes into your building

We do not switch brands mid-project. The BOQ specifies exact materials and grades — OPC 53 Grade cement, Fe 500D TMT bars, and approved sand from licensed quarries.

  • Cement: Ultratech, ACC, or Ramco (client choice at estimate stage)
  • Steel: JSW NeoSteel, Tata Tiscon, or SAIL
  • Waterproofing: Dr. Fixit or Fosroc systems
  • Electrical: Polycab or Havells (as per specification)
  • Plumbing: Astral or Supreme CPVC systems
